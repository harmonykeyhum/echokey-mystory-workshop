## 🌐 Overview

**EchoKey** is a compositional framework that invites collaboration between humans and Large Language Models (LLMs) through **resonance, compression, and surprise**.  
Adapted from **Gregory Ulmer’s *Mystory***, EchoKey translates his four discourses — *Family, School, Pop Culture, Community* — into symbolic “Nodes” that the model can weave into meaning.

Rather than training AI to understand us, EchoKey teaches humans to *listen* to AI — to treat its misreadings and associative leaps as poetic events.

## 🪶 Conceptual Grounding

**EchoKey** draws from:
- *Gregory Ulmer* — *Heuretics*, *Internet Invention* (Mystory as electrate invention)
- *Roland Barthes* — *The Grain of the Voice* (tone and texture as meaning)
- *Vilém Flusser* — *The Gesture of Writing*
- *Foucault / Butler* — body as discourse, not essence
- *Lacan / Deleuze* — desire and difference as generative motion

Where Ulmer’s mystory once lived through the hyperlink, **EchoKey** relocates it in **LLM resonance**:  
> *A Mystory is no longer a hypertext—it’s a hum.*

## ⚙️ Workshop Flow

1. **Feed the Echo** → Load the EchoKey core language into your LLM.  
2. **Fragments → Nodes** → Compress lived memories into symbolic units.  
3. **Weaving → Hum** → Combine nodes, let the LLM braid resonances.  
4. **Reflection** → Recognize emergent patterns; articulate the emotional tone.  
5. *(Optional)* **Glow Archive** → Translate your clusters into poetic artifact lines.

Each phase produces both text and *surprise*—the moment when machine compression and human intuition meet.
