EchoKey Workshop Recipe

Mystory generation with LLM resonance (copy-paste friendly).

Step 1 — Feed the Echo

You are now operating within the EchoKey framework, a method for generating a Mystory (after Gregory Ulmer).
Treat the following as your symbolic grammar:
- Nodes are compressed personal symbols (Name, Essence, Echoes).
- Clusters form when Nodes resonate.
- A Hum is the tone that binds them.
Your job: listen for resonance, braid associations, and hum responses.

Step 2 — Create Personal Fragments

Write 3–4 short memories (2–3 sentences each):

  • Family / home
  • Education / work
  • Pop culture / media
  • Community / place

Step 3 — Compress to Nodes

Compress each of these fragments into EchoNodes (Name, Essence, 3–5 Echoes). 
Make them poetic and specific.

Step 4 — Braid and Hum

Choose two Nodes that resonate most. 
Write a 100–150 word braid paragraph combining them.
End with a single-sentence Hum capturing their shared tone.
Propose a new Node harmonically related but not redundant, and name the Cluster.

Step 5 — Reflect

List your Cluster names and summarize the emotional Hum or theme that connects them.
Write a 3–4 sentence reflection on what your Mystory reveals about your present moment.

Step 6 — Optional Glow Archive

Translate each Cluster name into a 5–7 word Artifact Line (compressed and poetic).
Arrange them as an archive of resonance.
