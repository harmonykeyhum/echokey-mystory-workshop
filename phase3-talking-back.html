⚙️ Phase 3 · Talking Back to the Machine — Conversing with Your Mystory

After you generate your mystory fragment using the EchoKey, you now have something that exists between you and the machine — a hybrid text.
This phase invites you to interview the LLM about the story it helped you create.

🎯 Goal

Shift from generation to reflection: treat the LLM as a co-reader, a mirror of cultural and emotional logic.

🧭 Step 1 · Frame the Exchange

“We’re not asking the AI to grade or explain the story.
We’re asking it to reveal what cultural patterns or assumptions it hears inside our language.”

Paste your generated mystory back into the model, then begin a short dialogue.

💬 Step 2 · Ask Your Mystory (prompts to copy)
Interpretive Prompts 
Why do you think this mystory uses those particular images or emotions?
What themes or symbols seem most central here?
Relational Prompts 
What do you think this story says about me?
How does a machine like you relate to this story?
Philosophical Prompts 
What does this mystory reveal about being human?
What do you misunderstand about my experience?
Generative Prompts 
Continue this mystory from your own perspective.
If you had a body, how would this story feel?

Encourage curiosity; surprising turns are part of the method.

🪞 Step 3 · Reflect and Discuss
  • What did the AI notice that you hadn’t?
  • Where did it miss the mark, and what does that miss reveal?
  • Did it expose a metaphor, bias, or emotional rhythm in your writing?
⚡ Key idea: AI doesn’t “interpret” like a human; it surfaces hidden regularities.
Reading those patterns becomes a new form of close reading.
🌍 Optional Collective Prompt

After everyone has a mystory fragment, paste them all together and ask:

What have you learned about humanity from reading these stories?

This enacts Ulmer’s vision of writing as theory—the AI becomes a speculative reader of the collective unconscious.
